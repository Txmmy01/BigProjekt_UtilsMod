# UtilsMod Roadmap

## v2.0.0 planned modules

### Fullbright

- Independent module toggle
- Improves visibility in dark areas
- Does not alter block textures
- Can be enabled or disabled without affecting other modules

### Clear Lava

- Independent module toggle
- Improves visibility through lava by reducing its visual obstruction
- Does not change the lava block or fluid behavior
- Can be enabled or disabled without affecting other modules

### Clear Water

- Independent module toggle
- Improves underwater visibility by making water visually clearer
- Does not change water behavior or physics
- Can be enabled or disabled without affecting other modules

## Module requirements

- Every feature is a separate module in the UtilsMod GUI
- Each module has its own enable/disable control
- Modules operate independently and can be combined
- Settings are persistent across game restarts

## Later candidates

- HUD Utils
- Chat Utils
- Performance
- SoundMuter

## Release status

The modules listed under v2.0.0 are planned features. They are not implemented in v1.0.0.
